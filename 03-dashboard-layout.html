Round 3 · Dashboard Layout & Widgets

What does the home screen prioritize?

The Ops dashboard is the screen the majority of authenticated staff will see every time they log in. This round shapes which numbers lead, what chart anchors the page, what shows in the side rail, where today's schedule lives, how dense the surface feels, and what a brand-new business sees before they have data.

Six dimensions, ~6 minutes. Your picks are saved in your browser as you go. Hit Copy decision at the bottom when done and paste back to Phil.
1

Information priority (the 5 KPI cards)

What goes in the top-of-fold KPI strip. Five cards left-to-right. The order signals what "success" means at a glance every morning.

2

Hero visualization (the main chart)

The big chart that anchors the dashboard below the KPI strip. Sets the narrative — is this a business about money, traffic, growth, or operating efficiency?

3

"Today's Schedule" placement

Where the room-by-room calendar grid lives on the dashboard. The grid is the most info-dense element on the page — placement decides how much real-estate it claims.

4

Right-rail content

What lives in the narrow right column. Most apps use it for "things that need attention." Pick which type of attention should dominate.

5

Information density

How much breathing room everything gets. Affects spacing scale, padding, and the number of KPIs/rows that fit on a screen. Sets the tone for every page after dashboard.

6

Empty / first-day state

What a brand-new business sees when they log in before they have any members, transactions, or activity. This is the first impression of the product after sign-up.
