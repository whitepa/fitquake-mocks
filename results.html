Cross-round patterns & observations

Reviewer clustering

Across rounds, Phil + Mike cluster on the warmer / hero-ier / power-user dimensions (Round 1 5A/6A, Round 4 1A Growth + 2C Today). Craig leans calmer / operational / consensus-aware (Round 3 5C user-toggle, Round 1 5B/6B). Mike alone preferred lighter chrome in Round 1 (3B sidebar, 4B initials-only). Net effect: the locked design lands on the warmer end overall, with Craig's calmer preferences surfacing in genuinely safer-to-personalize areas (density).

The Round 3 → Round 4 pivot

Craig's persona-specific KPIs feedback (originating from ChatGPT research) reshaped how we think about dashboard content. Round 3 still answered useful layout questions (schedule placement, density, empty state) but its KPI picks became "generic fallback" — Round 4 carries the persona-flavored picks forward and seeds the per-role dashboard templates we'll ship in Phase 7i.

ChatGPT recommendations were 1-for-3

Round 4 tested whether reviewers would follow ChatGPT's recommendation packs (1A Growth for gym, 2A Income for trainer). The result: 1A Growth landed unanimously, but no reviewer picked 2A Income for trainers. Real users care about today's sessions and earnings, not long-arc revenue per client — at least at staff-trainer scale. The dashboard for an independent practitioner is a different product mode (2A would be its default). Worth keeping in mind when we ship trainer-onboarding: a single question ("Are you a staff trainer or independent?") could switch the dashboard preset.

One real product expansion (parked)

Phase 7j — Per-business header branding emerged from Phil's Round 2 comment on the business switcher. Each business gets its own logo + brand color in the header to confirm context. Parked in fitquake2/docs/PLAN.md at commit 01ec1f7. No schema churn (leverages Phase 6.5.G file storage); ~1 PW estimated.

"Compromise options" are real and worth designing for

Round 3 Dim 5 (density) ended up as a 1-1-1 split where each reviewer picked a different option — yet Craig's pick of 5C "User toggle" satisfies all three. Worth noting for future rounds: when a dimension is genuinely a personal preference (density, theme, default landing tab), offer a user-toggle option explicitly rather than forcing the team to settle on one default.
