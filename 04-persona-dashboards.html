Round 4 · Persona-specific Dashboards

Pick the right metrics for each persona

What "success" looks like differs by who's logging in. A gym manager cares about member growth and churn; a trainer cares about MRR and utilization; a member cares about their next class and account status. This round picks one KPI preset pack for each persona — these become the default dashboards we ship.

Three personas, three picks. Powered by Phase 6.5.H per-role widget placement — your picks here seed the default role templates. Individual users can still customize their layout later. ↳ Round opened in response to Craig's persona-specific metrics feedback (Round 3 themes).

What an owner or manager sees first when they log in. This is the "Is the business healthy?" glance. Heavy users: front-desk managers, regional ops, owners. ChatGPT recommendation (Craig's research): Growth-focused pack.

Trainer home Independent / staff trainer

What a trainer sees first. Their lens is their book of business — clients, sessions, income. Independent trainers want to know they're growing/keeping clients; staff trainers want today's schedule + their commission. ChatGPT recommendation (Craig's research): Income pack.

What a member sees when they log into their portal. Intentionally lighter — only 4 KPIs since members aren't running operations. The framing decides whether the portal feels like a service app (Activity), a banking app (Account), or a fitness journey app (Achievement).
