How a round works

  1. Open the round above.
  2. Click your preferred option on each dimension. Selected options get a teal ring + checkmark.
  3. Add a comment (optional) — useful for mix-ins like "1A palette but 1C logo," concerns, or trade-offs you want to flag.
  4. Hit Copy decision. Paste it back to Phil any way you like — email, Slack, text.
  5. If you want to change your mind, just come back. Your picks are saved in your browser.
