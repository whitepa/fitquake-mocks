Five role-adaptive views inside the unified app

Fitquake ships as one end-user app at /app/ serving every audience — members, trainers, gym staff, owners. The UI you see after login adapts to your highest role at the active business. There is no separate /admin/ or /portal/ app; all five views below render inside the same SPA, the same shell, the same auth state, the same business switcher. The Fitquake SaaS operator console (tenant management for Phil/Craig/Mike) will be a separate app at /console/ post-v1 — different audience, different IAM perimeter.

Login — single entry point for everyone

Decisions visible: R1 1A palette + 1D logo R2 1D full-bleed R2 4B footer link

Members, trainers, gym staff, owners — all log in here at app.fitquake.com/login. Full-bleed teal-deep brand background, white card centered. Logo above the form. "Trouble signing in? Reset password" link covers password + locked-account + email-issue cases in one place. After successful auth, the app resolves the user's highest role at their active business and redirects to the corresponding view.

Owner view of /app/

Decisions visible: R1 1A+1D · 2C cards · 3A sidebar · 4A avatars R2 5B switcher R3 2A revenue chart · 3B+4D side rail · 5C density R4 1A Growth KPIs

What Becky sees when her highest role at the active business is OWNER. Same shell as every other view (dark teal sidebar, header business switcher, 2C card surface, 4A avatars) — what changes is the KPI pack (Round 4 1A Growth: Net growth, Churn, MRR, Visits/member, Rev/member), the revenue trend hero chart (Round 3 2A), and the navigation items she has permission to see. If she's also a member of her own gym, the "View as..." menu lets her flip lenses without re-authenticating.

Loading & empty states

Decisions visible: R2 2C skeleton loading R3 6C minimal zeros empty state

Two boundary cases. Top: what shows during the moment between "page loaded" and "session resolved + data fetched" — the skeleton dashboard (Round 2 2C). Bottom: what a brand-new gym sees before any members, classes, or transactions exist — minimal zeros with inline action hints (Round 3 6C). Both honest, both fast-feeling, both lead to action.
